Before you collect your car

Two things to arrange before pick-up day.

1 Bring these with you

Please bring the following, in original:

  • Your driving licence
  • One address proof — Aadhaar, passport, utility bill or a recent bank statement

2 What to pay at pick-up

The first month's rent and the refundable advance are both due when you collect the vehicle.

Online Pay by Zelle to reddyfinancialsinc@gmail.com, then upload a screenshot using Add payment screenshot on this page.
In person Pay by cash at the counter. Our team will record it against your booking.
